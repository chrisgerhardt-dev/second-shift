Business Start-Up Advisory Services

High-Net-Worth Individual Tax Representation
Provided tax advisory and tax return preparation for hedge fund manager with 30 partnership and S-corporation interests, multi-state filings, carried interests, tax basis issues, multiple residences and U.S international tax reporting for foreign income and assets. Consulted on structuring of new investments, tax optimization, Charitable Lead Trusts, Private Foundations, and estimated tax planning.
Estate Representation for U.S. Family Facing Tax Penalties
Successfully represented U.S. family facing significant penalties for the failure to report $40 mm of foreign assets held in Swiss banks through various BVI, Panamanian and Hong Kong companies in collaboration with a leading international law firm. Beacon’s work in tracing the assets through multiple transfers over an eight-year period, creating financial statements for the offshore holding companies, and preparing amended tax returns for the family, was accepted by the IRS upon audit without change.
Billionaire’s Complex State Residency Audit
Represented billionaire private equity investor in complex state residency tax audit resulting in a reduction of over $6,000,000 of allocated income over a three year period through a novel database driven approach to income sourcing that the taxing agency accepted at the audit level.

Tax & Accounting Services For Leading Israeli Family Office
