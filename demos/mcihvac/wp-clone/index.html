    Quality in Design, Installation, & Service

    Since its establishment in 1948, Mechanical Contractors LLC., known as "MCI" among our customers, has consistently set the bar as the industry's gold standard in performance, reliability, and unwavering stability. We take immense pride in being the go-to provider for comprehensive building design and solutions.

    ​

    At MCI, we are driven to excel in our core capabilities, constantly pushing the boundaries of what is possible. We are thrilled to deliver unparalleled quality and services across various sectors, including commercial construction, engineering, project management, HVAC service & repair, building automation systems, and energy services.

    ​

    Our commitment to a full-service approach sets us apart. We offer our clients an extensive range of building solutions that not only cater to their needs but go above and beyond their wildest expectations. Above all, we hold true to the belief that our foremost responsibility is to "do right" by our customers. Drawing on our expertise, we tirelessly work to maximize the returns on every investment our customers make.

    When you choose MCI

    You're not just partnering with an industry leader; you're embarking on a journey of exceptional service and unparalleled dedication.  Trust us to exceed your expectations and provide you with the building solutions that will propel your success forward.

    How Can We Help?

    Construction

    From concept and design through full installation, we possess the expertise required for the entire commercial mechanical system of a building. Our dedicated Construction team is committed to delivering exceptional results and ensuring a hassle-free building experience for you.

    Engineering

    MCI boasts an exceptional in-house team of Professional Engineers, Senior Designers, Controls Engineers, and AutoCAD Specialists. These talented individuals excel not only in designing top-tier mechanical systems but are also at the forefront of industry innovation, introducing novel concepts in HVAC, piping, and plumbing design.

    Controls

    Automating your building's HVAC system can effectively reduce costs, saving both energy and money, while fostering a healthy and productive work environment. MCI has a proven track record of installing, maintaining, and repairing controls in various environments.

    Management

    Effective management is the cornerstone of a successful project. We are proud to have a team of highly competent and skilled project managers dedicated to ensuring the seamless operation of your project. From coordinating equipment deliveries to maintaining a steady flow of information to the general contractor, our project managers are industry-leading experts.

    Efficiency

    Our Energy Services team specializes in developing and supporting environmentally responsible strategies to minimize a building's energy consumption. In an era where energy is a precious resource, we can help you reduce your environmental footprint while saving money and upholding social responsibility.

    Service

    MCI Commercial Service technicians offer a 24/7 seamless customer experience. We service and repair all HVAC equipment brands, including pumps, air handlers, and air-cooled chillers. Implementing proactive HVAC system maintenance ensures efficient equipment operation throughout its lifespan, leading to reduced daily operating costs.
