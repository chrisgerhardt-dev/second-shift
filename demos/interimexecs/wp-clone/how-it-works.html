Assessing Your Leadership Needs

Maybe you know the exact type of leadership you need whether interim CEO, project-based CFO, fractional CMO, or temporary CIO. Or maybe you just know business isn’t running as well as it can, or you feel you can be doing things better.

Making the Right Match

Once we understand the resources needed and you give us the green light to move forward, we will match you with an executive or team to explore conversations. If there is chemistry and a fit with an executive, we draw up a contract and statement of work that defines how the executive will serve the company, team members, executives, board members, and shareholders.

Putting You on the Path to Growth and Success

The InterimExecs RED Team begins by doing a deep-dive into the business dynamics, looking at financials, operational processes, people, technology, market, positioning, culture, stakeholders, and more. After assessing what’s running smoothly and what isn’t working, the RED Team sets out a strategic roadmap and action plan. From there it is full speed ahead to implement the plan. We don’t believe in long reports that no one acts on…interim means immediate action and fast results.

Setting You Up for Future Wins

Interim executives empower the team and ensure a smooth handoff to the next permanent hire when the assignment comes to a close. In some cases, the interim’s mandate might be to train and mentor an internal resource to step into the role or hand off the keys when a specific project comes to completion. No matter what the end goal, the organization has a clear action plan to set them up for future success.
