Rapid Executive Deployment

Matching is the product. Deployment is the proof.

RED is how InterimExecs gets from a confidential call to an executive in the chair. Search services are free to companies.

01 · 270 Evaluation

Say where you need to go.

Maybe you know you need an interim CEO, project CFO, fractional CMO, or temporary CIO. Or you only know the business is not running as well as it can. The assessment names the skills and resources required.

02 · Green light

Nine-variable match.

The Best Fit Match Matrix© takes nine key criteria into account. InterimExecs has screened 5,000+ executives and selects the top 2% of interim, project, and fractional executives for on-call deployment.

03 · Review + SOW

Chemistry, then a contract.

You speak with the executive. If there is a fit, a statement of work defines how they serve the company, team, board, and shareholders.

04 · Deploy + handoff

Action, then a clean exit.

The RED Team deep-dives financials, operations, people, technology, market, and culture, then implements. When the assignment closes, they train an internal successor or help recruit the permanent hire.
