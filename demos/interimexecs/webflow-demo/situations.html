When an interim or fractional exec is the right move.

Interim management

For companies that are experiencing leadership gaps – CEO, CFO, COO, CIO, CTO, CMO, CSO – interim executives step in to get a company on track, ultimately hiring their permanent replacement. The average assignment ranges from 3 months – 9 months, but can go as long as 2+ years.

Engage

Part-time & fractional execs

Fractional executives take on multiple clients, especially with companies ramping up growth that are not ready to bring on a full-time hire. Companies get a full-powered executive for a fraction of the cost, and can scale time up or back.

Engage

Private equity operating executives

Private equity funds utilize the RED Team in portfolio companies seeking growth and returns — fill a gap, prepare for sale, or acquire and integrate. An interim COO can streamline operations. An interim CFO can upgrade reporting. An interim CIO can improve IT systems.

Engage

Project execution & implementation

When companies undergo specific initiatives, they draw on project executives without adding permanent overhead: technology (SAP, outsourcing, systems integration), operations, finance (IPO, M&A), marketing (product launch, market expansion).

Engage

Operational roadmap

Operating executives step in for a few days to a few weeks for a full view: financials, operations, marketing, people, process, sales. Then a roadmap a company can execute — and RED Team members who will implement, not just advise.

Engage

Turnaround, distress, restructuring

Revenues fall. Losses mount. Team morale plummets and key people leave. Turnaround specialists step in to fix or preserve assets — from minor storm clouds to catastrophic failure.
