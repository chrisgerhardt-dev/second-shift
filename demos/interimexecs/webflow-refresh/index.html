Questions companies ask

Interim management, explained

What is interim management?

Interim management is the rapid deployment of top senior managers and executives - sometimes in a matter of days - to help organizations navigate crucial changes, leadership gaps, or operational upgrades.

When does a fractional executive make sense?

Fractional executives are leveraged by companies on an on-demand and part-time basis, giving companies access to top senior executives for a fraction of the cost.

What is the length of interim executive assignments?

Usually a few months to over a year, depending on the needs of the company.
