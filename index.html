Night press · since the first shift ended

01 — The problem

The internet is a landfill of sites that are dying in public.

WordPress retainers. Plugin rot. Agencies that invoice for another month of not collapsing. Money leaves. The homepage still looks like 2014.

Second Shift is the adult in the room. We take over what you already have, or we rebuild it when you actually want a modern site. Either way, someone answers for it.

02 — Who it’s for

Two companies. Two paths.

Small companies

You do not want a Squarespace login.

You do not want a Webflow login either. You want a human who owns Tuesday — not a CMS you were supposed to “just update.”

That is Clone. Keep the current site. WordPress care. Changes come through us: chat or a person. Not a DIY editor you will ignore for eighteen months.

Mid-market and larger

When you want to edit it yourselves.

Then we move you to Webflow. Modern, owner-editable, without a committee of developers for a headline change.

Refresh if the bones are right. Reimagine if the whole thing has to move. Full rebrand is a different job.

03 — Pricing honesty

$750a month · growth desk

Not hosting with a smile. Not Squarespace at $16–$99.

Squarespace is DIY hosting. You get a login and a template. Clone is done-for-you care. Those stickers are not the same product, and we will not pretend they are so a spreadsheet can call us expensive.

The live monthly product is a growth desk: visible output on the site — pages, fixes, an SEO pass, work you can point at. It is not “we’re here if something breaks.”

Need the lights kept on and nothing else? Say so. That is a care conversation against what WordPress shops already charge. There is no cheaper button here because there is no cheaper live product. We will not invent one to win a price war.
